# Levels, the gun-bot and the playthrough (issue #6)

What each level of *T2: The Arcade Game* (Genesis) contains, how it ends, and how far the gun-bot
gets. The level script format is in `docs/subsystems/levels.md` (`tools/py/levelscript.py` decodes
the scripts); the object system in `docs/subsystems/objects.md`.

## The game in one table

There are **8 levels**. `level` (`$FF80CC`) counts 1..8; `$0A` is the high-score initials screen; 0
is attract/title (the attract demo plays levels 1..3 itself). Level 9 does not exist (its script
pointer is 0). The "state" column names the savestate taken when the level variable changed, at the
start of the intro text (`states/INDEX.md`, regenerated by `tools/harness/levels.sh`).

Failures and penalties: shooting a human calls `SetPlayerState(6)` ("HUMAN CASUALTY", the humans
counter `+$38` feeds the end-of-level "human casualties penalty"); energy 0 = "TERMINATED", then
the continue countdown (credits), or game over and the initials screen when the score is high enough.
Levels 3, 7 and 8 have their own failure ("John Connor has perished", "Try again") that restarts the
section instead of costing a life.

## The gun-bot

`bot N` / `bot until LEVEL MAX` in the headless harness (`tools/harness/bot.c`, commands in the
`tools/harness/harness.c` header). Every frame it reads the object table, picks a target and drives the
Menacer (or a pad crosshair: the bot steers it with the d-pad) for one or both players:

* **Hit boxes** are learned through the cpu hook: when `HitBox` `$005940` runs it reads the object at
  `(2,a6)` and the box table at `(a3)`, so every shot reveals the current box of every object.
  Objects not box-tested lately get a 24x40 box above their (X, Y).
* **Occlusion** as `ShotHitTest` decides it: of all boxes under the crosshair the lowest depth byte
  (+7) takes the hit. The bot aims only at points where its target is in front (the level-1 tank
  body covers its own arms, for example), and fires only when the object under the crosshair is a
  shootable non-human.
* **Humans** are never aimed at or fired over: the hit handlers that end in "HUMAN CASUALTY"
  (`$005F04`, `$0065FC`, `$006652`), plus any handler hit just before a player's humans counter
  (+$38) rises (learned). Zero human casualties in all 35 campaign runs.
* **Danger**: an object whose AI routine (`ObjRunAll` reads `(+$20,a6)` at `$00B3B2`) reaches
  `PlayerDamage` `$0090B2` marks its type dangerous; dangerous types are shot first. On escort levels
  enemies near the truck / van come first. Every other 300 frames the bot switches to "big targets
  first" (hit points), so bosses behind endless swarms (the level-4 CPU) still go down.
* **Armour**: an object that took 20 hits without losing hit points gets missiles instead (the
  T-1000). Missiles also go to targets with >= 40 hp and to crowds of 6+ (keeping 5 in reserve).
* **Gunpower**: the trigger is tapped (1 frame on, 1 off), which keeps gunpower full and fires twice
  as often as holding (`docs/gun.md`).
* **Continue**: Start when the player's state is dying/waiting; **initials**: shoots A, B, C, ENTER.
* **Cheats** (each marks the run CHEATED in `bot.log`): `skip N` (level-skip cheat through pause +
  B+C after N frames), `energy 1`, `protect 1` (level 3: the truck's damage cells stay >= 1; level 7:
  the helicopter hit count stays at 5), `freeze 1` (level 8: sets the T-1000 frozen flag; not enough,
  see below).

## How far it gets

Honest (no cheat at all, `honest_gun.txt` / `honest_pad.txt`, 4 credits):

| run | level 1 | level 2 | level 3 | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1P Menacer | cleared (26000 frames, 0 deaths) | cleared (11600 frames) | 5 deaths: the truck is lost again and again | game over on level 3, score 1 705 000, initials entered |
| 1P joypad | cleared (1 death) | cleared | 4 deaths | game over on level 3, score 1 498 900 |

So the honest bot clears **levels 1 and 2** and fails the level-3 escort: it does not protect the
truck well enough (endoskeletons walk in from the screen edges where the boxes are guesses).

With the energy and protect cheats (`levels.sh full`, `full2p`, `fullpad`), levels **1 to 7** are
played to their real end in all three modes (frames per level, 1P Menacer: 26000, 11600, 8700,
19000, 13900, 8700, 13900). **Level 8 is not won**: the T-1000 has to stand in a nitrogen leak for
about 1400 frames; the bot makes leaks in his column but cannot keep him there, and setting the
frozen flag only starts the second phase, which ends in "John Connor has perished" and a retry.
The ending was therefore reached with a cheat (`ending.txt`: `level_exit = 1` on level 8, what the
level's last object does), in both text variants.

Every level, the high-score screen and the attract return were also visited with the level-skip
cheat in all four modes (`chain_skip_*.txt`), which is where the states come from.

## Open

* Level 3 honestly: better boxes for objects entering from the edges, earlier missiles near the truck.
* Pickups (`pickup_pool` `$FF9AB2`: weapons, energy, smart bomb) are not shot on purpose yet.
